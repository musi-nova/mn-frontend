How does it work?

Log in to Musi-Nova

Launch a campaign on your Musi-Nova dashboard

Add the link to the playlist you created. Our system will analyse your playlist and send the feedback

Improve your playlist based on the feedback and use the tools that we provide to create a stunning playlist’s cover

Decide how much you want to spend on promotion and for how many days

Within 24 hours, we will create your ad and start the promotion (during the working days)

Check the results of the promotion at your Musi-Nova dashboard

See how your followers and listeners grow!
